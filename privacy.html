Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 May 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what data A Dollar of Discipline collects, why, and your rights over it. We collect as little as possible, and we never sell your data.

1. Data we collect

  • Account data: email address, optional display name.
  • Goal data: current weight, target weight, stake amount, weigh-in window.
  • Weigh-in data: the numeric reading parsed from your scale photo, the timestamp, and whether the weigh-in resulted in a charge.
  • Photo data: the scale photo you submit nightly. By default photos are processed on-device and only the parsed number is stored on our servers. If you opt in to cloud backup, the photos are stored encrypted.
  • Payment data: handled by our payment processor (Stripe or Apple). We never see or store your full card number.
  • Diagnostic data: anonymous crash reports and basic usage metrics so we can fix bugs.

2. How we use your data

  • To run the App: log weigh-ins, calculate streaks, and trigger charges according to your settings.
  • To send the single nightly reminder.
  • To respond when you contact support.
  • To debug and improve the App.

We do not use your data for advertising, profiling, or training third-party AI models.

3. Who we share data with

  • Payment processors — to handle charges securely.
  • Cloud hosting providers — to store your account data, encrypted in transit and at rest.
  • Legal authorities — only when required by valid legal process.

We do not sell your data. Ever.

4. Data retention

Account and goal data are retained while your account is active. When you delete your account, we delete your personal data within 30 days, except for the minimum financial records we are legally required to retain.

5. Security

We use industry-standard encryption in transit (TLS) and at rest. Payment data is tokenized by our processor. No system is perfectly secure — please use a strong unique password and report suspected breaches to wadud@atomicmail.io.

6. Your rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or export your personal data, and to object to certain processing. To exercise these rights, email wadud@atomicmail.io. We respond within 30 days.

7. Children

The App is not intended for users under 18. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child has provided us data, contact us and we will delete it.

8. International transfers

Your data may be processed in countries other than your own. Where required, we rely on standard contractual clauses or equivalent safeguards.

9. Changes to this Policy

We will notify you in-app of material changes. Continued use of the App after a change indicates acceptance.

10. Contact

Privacy questions or data requests: wadud@atomicmail.io.
