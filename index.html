Why it works

A commitment device, not a coach.

We don't cheer. We don't nag. You write the contract — we hold you to it.

Any discipline you can prove

Prayer, workouts, study, wake-up, bedtime, weight goals — or write your own. If it can be shown with a photo or short video, you can commit to it.

You pick the schedule

Choose the exact days and times that fit your life. Daily, weekdays only, three times a week — your call. A real alarm fires on your lock screen at the moment you chose.

Proof, verified automatically

When the alarm fires, you have a window you set — usually fifteen minutes — to submit a photo or video. Our verifier checks it actually shows what you committed to.

A real penalty for skipping

Pick what missing costs you. From a dollar to whatever stings enough to keep you honest. Miss the window or fail verification and your card is charged automatically. No exceptions, no refunds.

Completely private

Your proof is used for verification, full stop. No social feed, no leaderboard, no one watching. The contract is between you and your future self.

Streaks that actually mean something

Hit three, seven, fourteen, thirty, sixty, a hundred, three hundred sixty-five in a row and we'll mark it — quietly. Skip a day, your streak resets. That's the whole point.

How it works

Four steps. Then it's just showing up.

Set the contract once. The rest is muscle memory.

01 · CHOOSE

Pick your discipline

Choose from built-in habits — prayer, workout, study, sleep, weight, wake-up — or write a custom one with your own proof description.

02 · COMMIT

Set the terms

Pick your days, your time, how long your proof window stays open, and what missing costs you. The harder the habit, the higher the stake.

03 · LOCK IN

Connect a card

Link a payment method securely. This is what turns a goal into a commitment. Cards are only charged after a missed or failed proof.

04 · SHOW UP

Submit proof. Or pay.

When your alarm fires, you have your window. Submit a photo or video — we verify it. Miss the window or fail verification and your penalty is charged automatically.
